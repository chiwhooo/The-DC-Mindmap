It starts with the opening of a gala. It's being hosted by an individual called [[Albert Noyce]]. It's quickly interrupted by [[Roy Harper]] and [[Oliver Queen]] in vigilante gear crashing through the glass ceiling as they chase after [[Werner Zytle]], also known as Count Vertigo. They subdue him and are quickly met with applause from the attendees.

Albert tries to introduce himself to Green Arrow, but Oliver refuses the handshake due to the man causing an overdose epidemic by pushing addictive pain meds. Albert corrects him by saying that his company was cleared of all wrong doing legally, and that people make their own choices. Oliver starts to go off on him, but Roy steps in to deescalate. 

As the two dart off and head back towards Oliver's apartment, they start to chat. They reminisce and bond over the past as Green Arrow and Speedy. Roy thanks Oliver for the patrol, Oliver tells Roy he is proud of him and Roy leaves.

As Roy walks home, he stumbles upon an addicted beggar. Roy is about to offer some type of aid, but when he looks at the man all he can see is a reflection of his own addiction as Speedy and walks away. He runs into a man who's been shot through the chest with an arrow. A man called [[Crimson Archer]] appears and reveals he's the one who did it, when Roy tries to restrain him; he's similarly shot. Inside the arrow is a liquid called 'The Crimson Sand' which quickly starts to impact Roy's consciousness as it resembles an overdose.
