The comic starts with [[Tai Pham]] drawing in his grandmother's, [[Kim Tran]], shop; [[The Jade Market]]. The scene is interrupted by a brick being thrown the window from a yellow truck before they drive off, for the 3rd time in 2 months. It appears to be a racist attack, with a message scrawled on the brick to 'GO HOME!'.

Later at dinner Tai's parents, [[Long Pham]] and unnamed mother, express concern over this situation. They tell Kim that maybe she should consider selling and also talk about Tai being kept away from this situation. Kim says no, she won't sell and storms off.

That night Tai is woken from a nightmare as Kim's power ring hits his head and startles him awake. Tai goes to return it to Kim, but it comes back once again. At his second attempt returning it, Kim wakes up and tells Tai that it's his ring now before falling asleep again. Tai brushes off the incident but takes the ring with him. When he goes to check on her the next morning, she has passed away from old age in her sleep.

At the funeral we are introduced to [[Serena Walker]] and [[Tommy]], Tai's friends who have come to support him at the funeral. We are given backstory on Kim, that she was a pinnacle of the Vietnamese community and gave support to everyone who needed it. [[Xander Griffin]], supposed billionaire, also stops by to pay his respects and insists that if Tai needs it he should call Xander for help. 

By the start of the next week, things are returning back to normal despite their grief. Tai's sisters, [[Bee Pham]] and [[Lan Pham]], are returning back to university and wish him well before they leave. In school, Tai and Serena decide to do a project on Xander due to Tai's connection to the man. That day after school Tai is in his room when his ring informs him about low charge, much to his shock. It then proceeds to drag him to where their power lantern is kept, resembling a Hoi An lantern. He reads out the oath Kim had written for him and is subsequently teleported to a construct of [[Oa]].

As he tries to get his bearings, [[John Stewart]] comes down to greet Tai and teach him about the ring but is also shocked by the fact that Tai is still only 13. John tells Tai that Kim was like a mentor to him, and tells him about Kim's secret life as a Green Lantern. Kim then appears as a spirit to tell Tai about why she did this and what her life was like with the ring. She ensures Tai that he was the right pick before Tai wakes up from his summoning.

When leaving the room, he finds Xander in the front of the store. Xander had paid to have the window replaced and has put in an offer to buy the Jade Market. Tai requests a meeting with Xander for his class project but Xander confirms Serena already had set up a day for them to do it before he leaves.

That night as Tai sleeps, John wakes him up with a knock on the window and tells him they are going to train. He takes Tai to the moon to practice, with the help of fellow Green Lantern, Queen [[Iolande]].
