We start with a flashback of [[Kenny Gordon]] talking to his mother's, [[Brenda Gordon]], grave. He monologues and apologises for what he is going to do with the rest of the [[Freshwater]] survivors. It then cuts to Kenny in prison, receiving a visit from [[Oliver Queen]].

At first Kenny is going to refuse, but Oliver convinces him to hear him out. Oliver apologises for what he did and reveals that he funded an organisation called the 'Brenda Gordon Environmental Group' which aims to tear down Freshwater and rebuild it using renewable energy sources. Kenny doesn't say thank you, but it's clear he appreciates the gesture as he ends the meeting.

[[Lena 'Benny' Benítez]] walks Oliver out of the prison so she can ask some questions. She asks about why he ever worked for Horton Chemicals, and Oliver says he only took it to appease a dying friend's wish; he left as soon as he could and left it in the hands of someone he believed he could trust, 'Max'.

When she points out that Oliver was being surprisingly lax about his attempted murder, Oliver goes off on her. He talks vaguely about his time on the island and how he would have done the same. Benny remarks that he reminds her of someone she met recently (Green Arrow). The conversation ends with Benny giving Oliver her business card and telling him to call her if he needs to talk.

We cut to a shot of [[Roy Harper]], [[Dinah Lance]] and [[Connor Hawke]] all gathered around a dinner table as Connor cooks dinner for the group. They are waiting for Oliver to arrive, as he's running late. Oliver calls to let Dinah know why he's running late before he breaks into the office building of Horton Chemicals while dressed as Green Arrow. He tackles the CEO Max to the ground and threatens him over the damage he caused to the people of Freshwater. Oliver gives Max a list of names before telling him to invest and provide for these people or else. He leaves after that and returns to his family dinner.
