The comic starts with [[Bartholomew 'Barry' Allen]] narrating what's happening, reflecting on the cruise ship he's currently on and how it is starting to rapidly sink. He admits he has no idea on how to solve this before we cut back to the past.

Barry had been given a cruise trip as a result of his PD work, and had a plus one invitation. He takes the chance to invite [[Iris West]], the woman he really likes (but is not currently dating in this volume). While aboard the ship, the two are attending a formal ball when [[King Shark]] crashes the event and demands that everyone desert the ship. When he goes to attack Iris, Barry reappears in his Flash suit and tries to fight him off. King Shark easily takes the hits and instead goes to leave a hole in the hull and try to sink the ship instead.

Now in the present, Barry still has no idea what to do. He's unsure if he'll be able to run all the passengers to shore in time before the ship sinks and isn't willing to take that risk. King Shark takes advantage of Barry's distraction to drag him back under the water, Barry manages to escape and remembers a weakness of sharks is their weak eyesight. He stuns King Shark with the help of the crew and manages to knock King Shark down.

Iris points out how out of the blue this attack was, and Barry agrees. King Shark speaks up, admitting his defeat but stating that the cruise line poisons the water by dumping their trash in international waters and just paying the fine. Barry promises to expose the cruise's crimes and finds a way using high-speed friction to weld metal to the hole and prevent the boat from sinking.

Barry tries to apologise to Iris for the failure of a date, but she brushes it off and says it was a great time.
