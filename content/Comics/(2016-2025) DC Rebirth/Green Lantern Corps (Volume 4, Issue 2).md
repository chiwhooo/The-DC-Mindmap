[[John Stewart]], [[Kilowog]], [[Razer]] and [[Shayera Thal]] are flying towards [[Thangar]] at the request for political help, to which we find the planet half destroyed and falling apart. Shayera discusses the impact of the Red Lantern's powers who caused this, which prompts Razer and Kilowog to talk about how the destruction of the energy batteries has made controlling their emotions and powers even more difficult. However, the conversation is interrupted by a [[Red Lantern Corps]] ship.

We cut to the point of view of [[Atros 'Atrocitus']], discussing how the Green Lantern's took everything from him. [[Dexter 'Dex-Starr']] comes in and tells Atrocitus that their drill has pierced through Thanagar, much to Atrocitus's pleasure.

Back to the Green Lantern team, we see [[Bleez]], Nite-Lik and another unidentified [[Red Lantern]] around the outside of ship. John tells Kilowog and Razer to follow the trio while himself and Shayera sneak in to look inside the ship. John and Shayera temporarily have to hide inside a tight closet while Dex-Starr walks past before they continue on exploring again.

When we return to Kilowog and Razer, they are dutifully following the trio who are investigating the drilled breach of the ruined planet. Bleez reports back to Atrocitus that they have found another piece of [[Nth Metal]].

Switching P.O.V's again we see Atrocitus realising there are intruders in the ship and setting off an alarm, John and Shayera start to rush through the ship and discover the presence of [[Zilius Zox]].
