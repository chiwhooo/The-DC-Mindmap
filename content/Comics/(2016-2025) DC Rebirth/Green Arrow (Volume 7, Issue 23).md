It is confirmed that [[Kenny Gordon]] is the originator of the [[Freshwater Killer]] group: wearing gas masks as a symbol of the poison they were breathing in; a red raincoat as a representation of little red riding hood and how they were also lied to and sold a fairy-tale and finally a pipe as a weapon, the same pipes that were pumping deadly water into their houses. Kenny confirms that their plan of killing those who poisoned them is by using the same water that was used on them.

We cut to the present again as [[Oliver 'Ollie' Queen]] approaches [[Freshwater Community School]], where there appears to be a base of operations for the Freshwater Killer group. He stealthily knocks out the front guard before grappling to the top of the building and stumbling upon [[Benny Benítez]] who is currently being dangled above the poisonous water and lowered down by Kenny in costume. Oliver interrupts and cuts Benny free from the ropes and proceeds to take down the rest of the group apart from Kenny, he attempts to convince the man to stop but is taken off guard and knocked down. It looks like Kenny is about to kill Oliver before Benny steps in and and knocks him unconscious.

Benny asks Oliver if he's okay, and Oliver muses about the people of the Freshwater Killer group; and how there is no okay for them, they had been failed by the system.
