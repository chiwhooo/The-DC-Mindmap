[[Oliver Queen]] wakes up to a late night call from [[Dinah Lance]] who tells him that [[Roy Harper]] is in hospital. Oliver rushes over to the hospital and finds Dinah and [[Lian Harper]] in the room with Roy unconscious in bed. Dinah tells Oliver that they believe it's an overdose, to which Oliver is in shock and denies the idea that Roy could be using again. Oliver comforts Lian who is in a lot of distress over her dad, and promises that the whole family will be there for each other. Dinah and Lian go on a walk and leave Roy and Oliver alone.

Oliver sits with Roy and reminisces on the mistakes he made when he first discovered Roy's addiction. The doctor comes in to talk to Oliver and Oliver shouts at the doctor that it's impossible for Roy to have used drugs and that the doctor is forming a unbiased opinion based on Roy's actions as a teen. The doctor manages to calm Oliver down, and tells him that Roy is lucky to be just in a coma rather than dead. Oliver manages to pry the information about where Roy was found and goes to investigate the alley, finding the arrow he was shot with.

Oliver is getting dressed as Green Arrow to go out when Lian knocks on the door to talk to him. Lian asks to come with Oliver, but Oliver says no and tells him to spend time with Roy instead as he needs her support right now. Lian relents and Oliver leaves.

[[Lena 'Benny' Benítez]] is walking her dog, Arrow through a park when Green Arrow approaches and tells her (without any identifying features) about Roy and what he's found. Benny confirms that the police have been finding these everywhere in the presence of overdoses, mostly targeting drug dealers. Benny shows Oliver a found video of the [[Crimson Archer]] attacking another person and tells him the villain's name.

Lian walks back to the hospital, running into Dinah on the way out. She goes back through the hospital to Roy's room but this time the bed is empty with Roy nowhere to be seen.
