The comic starts on a scene from 'months ago' from the perspective of [[Slade Wilson]] and how he came back to life at the hands of [[Tara Markov]]. He remarks on his relationship with Tara; ex-protégé, sometimes friend and mostly aggressive rival. She reveals that [[Amanda Waller]] has been trying to gain control over Superheroes and Vigilantes by forming a team made up of their enemies and using bombs in their heads to keep them there. Tara says that she saved Slade to ensure that she has someone on her side when she breaks free of Amanda's control, and if it comes to it she can take Slade down again with her.

We return to the present, featuring: [[Richard 'Dick' Grayson]], [[Roy Harper]], [[Donna Troy]], [[Garfield 'Gar' Logan]], [[Victor 'Vic' Stone]], [[Koriand'r 'Starfire']], [[Rachel 'Raven' Roth]] and [[Vanessa 'Vanadia']] as they investigate a prison break (of character [[Caitlin Snow]]) on a convoy in New York City. Vic and Donna have a heart-to-heart regarding Vic going behind her back to rebuild Vanadia's cyborg body and any bad blood regarding the matter is put to rest. He deduces that the plan was concocted by [[William Tockman]], [[Baran Flinders]] and Slade. 

Slade had decided to leave behind [[Roger Hayden]] and [[Volcano Man]], with Volcano Man appearing and attacking the Titans upon their approach. Vanadia provides information on Volcano Man she shouldn't have had access to, knowing things the rest of the team didn't (to Vic and Donna's concern). Vic manages to get him into the ocean and causes him to rapidly cool as a result, ending the fight. 

Donna and Roy decide to have a 'private chat' about recent events between them later before we pan to Gar and Rachel. Rachel checks on Gar about the rage she can sense from him, and Gar admits that he is feeling a lot of anger around Slade coming back to life; considering the idea that maybe he should be dead after all he had done.
