The issue opens with rats overrunning the city, [[Bartholomew 'Barry' Allen]] can instantly tell that this is the work of [[Hartley Rathaway]], also known as Pied Piper and a member of the [[Flash Rogue Gallery]]. Barry moves to interrogate fellow Rogue members [[Mick Rory]] (Heat Wave), [[Samuel 'Sam' Scudder]] (Mirror Master) and [[Axel Walker]] (Trickster), who refuse to provide any information. [[Leonard 'Len' Snart]] (Captain Cold) ends up accidentally revealing too much information and giving Barry a hint to Hartley's whereabouts.

Barry manages to find Hartley playing in park, surrounded by screens and broadcasting his sonic frequencies across [[Central City]]. Barry immediately starts to fight Hartley, but Hartley reveals that the reason he's doing this is to help [[Graciella Sava]], a missing girl who even the police have given up on.

As Barry hears this story, he can't help but reflect on his own past and how his father was arrested for a crime he didn't commit. In a similar way to how Hartley is being accused now by Barry. Barry apologises and returns the flute, offering to help. Hartley continues playing until the rats are able to pinpoint the location of Graciella to which Barry goes after her.
