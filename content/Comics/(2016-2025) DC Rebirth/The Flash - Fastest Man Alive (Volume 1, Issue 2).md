We start 6 hours ago, from an unknown narrator. They say it's T-minus 7 hours until an event, and that whoever is sending them from wherever they are coming from has thousands of clones to send until someone lives through the travel. They seem to be struggling with the side-effects and their translator isn't working, yet when police approach the unknown narrator promptly attacks and beats them. After that, she peels off her mask and reveals herself to be Lieutenant [[Beth Arden]], using the moniker Shift, and that her sole goal is to kill the Flash.

A short switch to [[Bartholomew 'Barry' Allen]]'s point of view and the present, Barry (while dressed as Flash) is trying to help the civilians of Central City after an unknown attacker (Beth) is terrorising the city, with specifically targeting kids. Barry concludes that rules out most of his rogues and is confused on how he has yet to even spot the villain who is doing this.

We switch to 4 hours prior while Barry is at work as a CSI, investigating the remains of 4 bodies found which look to have been ran-over on the train tracks. It's strange because the train track has been decommissioned for months with no trains coming through. Barry keeps investigating and realises that it's likely they died due to a malfunctioning rollercoaster an, giving a strong lead on the case. He's commended for his work before we go back to the present.

Barry is still struggling to find the villain, searching fruitlessly through Central City. He takes a moment to pause and a homing missile starts to chase after him, it's fast enough to even gain on him. It ends up tagging him and sending him tumbling, Beth appears at this point and tells Barry she's going to kill him because he killed her world.

We get a brief flashback of Beth from 5 hours ago and her planning to how to kill Barry. She seems at ease with the fact that she will likely die in pursuit of killing him, but still determined.

She informs Barry she is apart of the [[Speed Force Resistance]] and that he is her prisoner, when Barry tries to escape she manages to attack him. Barry decides to take the offensive and hits her first, but her armour allows her to dodge the attack. Barry realises all of her abilities are weapons and technology, which allows them to be out thought. He manages to destroy her trident and promises he will fix whatever he has done wrong as long as she peacefully talks to him.

Beth reveals [[Eobard Thawne]] is the one who has been slaughtering millions of people who he believed worshipped Barry. Beth makes Barry promise to help her defeat him when the time comes before automatically being teleported back to her timeline. The comic ends with it being confirmed that Barry was right about the carnival, but he can't focus beyond the fact that he might be the reason the world ends in the future.
