[[Harold 'Hal' Jordan]] finds himself heading along the road till he hits ocean, asking some fisherman for work. They agree and he finds himself helping out catching crabs.

Switching to [[Guy Gardner]]'s point of view, he is thinking about how incredible his life is with the power ring. And yet he can't stop thinking of Hal and how foolish he must be to not embrace this lifestyle. After a while of lingering on the thought, Guy decides to walk about the streets of New York instead. He wanders into a tattoo shop owned by [[Abel Tarrant]], a prior villain of Hal's who had since decided to return to normal life. When Guy starts poking at him and harassing him into trying to show off his powers, Abel kicks him out. After Abel takes a minute to recompose himself, Guy appears again and keeps mocking him. It ends up starting a fight, with Abel flying after Guy. Guy remarks himself a genius and keeps leading him in the direction of Hal.

Hal meanwhile is making friends with his co-workers and settling into the idea of settling where he's staying for now, when his ring alerts him to the fact that Guy is rapidly approaching. Hal takes off, shifting into Green Lantern gear to try and get Guy to leave him alone.
