The comic starts with [[Harold 'Hal' Jordan]] reminiscing on his current life, dealing with his despair with his current life before flying to the current [[Justice League of America]] headquarters. He runs into [[Bruce Wayne]] who reminds Hal that he is always welcome to join. [[Theodore 'Ted' Kord]], [[Beatriz 'Fire' da Costa]] and [[Tora 'Ice' Olafsdotter]] encourage Hal to join and replace [[Guy Gardner]] as the current Green Lantern on the team (much to Guy's annoyance). Hal refuses and cites the fact that he's busy, however Bruce privately questions this and Hal admits that he's been feeling stressed from his life as a superhero and is taking a break from it all to re-adjust to normal life before leaving again.

We get a single page featuring [[John Stewart]], who seems to be grappling with his own problems regarding the Green Lantern title before switching back to Hal.

Hal trainhops onto a oncoming cargo train, pondering his life so far and how it seems to have changed for the worst; with people not needing Green Lantern anymore. Train security finds him and chucks him back out, Hal nearly uses his power ring but forces himself to not and declares he has somewhere else to go before storming off.

Hal ends up hitchhiking to [[Hope Springs]], a spot he had passed through with [[Oliver Queen]] during their [[Hard Travelling Heroes]] days. Hal makes it into town and searches for a woman called [[Rose Hardin]] (Rose Lewis when he last saw her), he manages to find her and discovers that she's now a poor widowed farmer who is trying to raise her son by herself. Hal convinces her to let him work for food and a bed only, as she has no money to spare on employment.

John is going a debate with himself regarding his status as a Green Lantern, asking the sky if he's truly a Lantern after he made a mistake and got people killed. There is no response once again from the [[Guardians of the Universe]] so instead John decides to fly to [[Oa]] to get answers himself.

Rose teaches Hal about how to hoe the fields and take care of the plants, without relying on sheer force alone. As night falls, Rose's son [[Toby Hardin]], comes out to talk to Hal about the stars. when Hal starts to talk about the aliens he has met in his adventures as Green Lantern; Rose comes out and scolds him, telling Hal it's not practical to fill his head up with such nonsense. Hal agrees and we return back to John.

Oa is still destroyed and a ruin of what once was, however one of the guardians had promised they would stay. When John goes searching for them, he finds the corpse of prior Green Lantern [[Priest]] murdered. The page ends on the bloodied body.

Hal is back at Rose's farm, working away when Guy comes around and starts to bother Hal; picking at him about how times change and provoking a fight. When Hal uses his ring to get Guy to back off, Rose and Toby spot the scene. When Hal rushes to try and explain, Rose says that he isn't a man anymore; something beyond that and demands Hal leaves. Hal agrees without argument and heads back to the road.
