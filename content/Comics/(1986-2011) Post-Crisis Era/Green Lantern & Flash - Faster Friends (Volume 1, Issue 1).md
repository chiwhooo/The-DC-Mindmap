The comic starts out with this paragraph on the opening page:

Decades ago a callous government forced a ring-wielding guardian and a scarlet speedster to team up against an "alien menace". Green Lantern and Flash put their differences aside and discovered the enemy sometimes does not come from without. The friendship formed that day has spanned the years. Now that same alien has abducted the Golden Age heroes ([[Alan Scott]] and [[Jason 'Jay' Garrick]]). The new Green Lantern ([[Kyle Rayner]]) and Flash ([[Wallace 'Wally' West]]) must join forces to save their original counterparts - even if it means defeating them - and, hopefully, the world. If they can only get along for five minutes.
