Kim was born in Vietnam, but was a refugee and escaped the country to America. The boat ride to America was very dangerous, and she had to use her Green Lantern powers to protect her family and the other refugees on the boat with her. She never told her family about her role as a Green Lantern to protect them as well as that the pain was easier to keep in the past.
